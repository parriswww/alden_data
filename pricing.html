Shared conditions

How every option works

  • Original typed PDFs with embedded text only. Photos, image-only scans, OCR processing, and handwriting extraction are not supported.
  • One invoice per PDF.
  • Usage counts unique successfully processed invoices and original PDF pages. Unchanged duplicates and retries caused by Alden errors are not counted again. Revised documents count as new.
  • Monthly allowances reset at renewal. At the limit, work is held pending your approval. No automatic extra charges.
  • Your team reviews exceptions and makes all business decisions.
  • New work is quoted before it begins.
  • Business days, service hours, and cutoff times are agreed during onboarding.
  • Turnaround targets start after complete eligible inputs, payment, sample approval, and the agreed start date.
  • Allowances and turnaround targets are subject to sample validation and confirmed in your written scope. They are not guarantees.
  • Any required customer-owned software and separately payable third-party costs are disclosed before purchase.
  • All prices are in USD. Every option starts with a conversation, not a checkout.
